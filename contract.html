1Scope of Services

The Coding Pit agrees to provide AI and technology consulting services, which may include any combination of the following as outlined in the selected service tier:

  • AI strategy consultation and workflow analysis
  • Custom AI tool design, configuration, and integration
  • Automation of repetitive documentation and reporting tasks
  • Staff training and onboarding on AI tools
  • Ongoing support, maintenance, and optimization
  • Third-party software evaluation and recommendations

Specific deliverables for each engagement phase will be documented in a separate Statement of Work (SOW) attached to this agreement.
